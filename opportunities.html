Join LignoBio Lab

Opportunities

We welcome motivated students, early-career researchers and collaborators interested in lignocellulosic biomass valorisation, enzyme technology, biofuels, biocatalysis, environmental biotechnology and sustainable biorefineries.

Learn, Explore and Build Sustainable Technologies

Research in LignoBio Lab combines fundamental biochemistry and enzymology with applied biotechnology and bioprocess development. Students are encouraged to understand the underlying science while developing practical solutions for sustainable utilisation of renewable biological resources.
