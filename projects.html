Research in Action

Projects & Capabilities

Our research programme integrates biomass pretreatment, enzymology, microbial biotechnology, fermentation, nanobiocatalysis, materials development and sustainability assessment to create value from lignocellulosic resources.

Integrated Research Programme

Connecting Fundamental Biochemistry with Sustainable Biorefinery Development

LignoBio Lab investigates lignocellulosic biomass as a renewable platform for producing fuels, enzymes, chemicals and functional materials.

Our projects are designed to address different stages of the biomass conversion chain, including feedstock pretreatment, enzymatic deconstruction, fermentation, recovery of individual biomass fractions and development of value-added products.

An important emerging objective is to integrate these operations into complete biorefinery systems that maximise resource utilisation while considering economic and environmental sustainability.

Flagship Programmes

Major Research Projects

Representative research programmes that define the current and emerging scientific direction of LignoBio Lab.

PROJECT 01

Integrated Pretreatment of Lignocellulosic Biomass

Development of chemical, microwave-assisted, surfactant-assisted and deep eutectic solvent-based strategies for improving delignification, fractionation and enzyme accessibility of agricultural residues.

Alkali Microwave Surfactants DES Delignification
PROJECT 02

Second-Generation Bioethanol from Agricultural Residues

Integration of biomass pretreatment, enzymatic saccharification and microbial fermentation for improving fermentable-sugar recovery and bioethanol production from non-food lignocellulosic feedstocks.

2G Bioethanol Saccharification Fermentation SSF Process Optimisation
PROJECT 03

Microbial Enzymes for Biomass Deconstruction

Production, optimisation and application of plant cell wall degrading enzymes, including cellulases, xylanases and pectinases, for improved deconstruction of complex lignocellulosic substrates.

Cellulase Xylanase Pectinase PCWDEs Biocatalysis
PROJECT 04

Enzyme Immobilisation & Nanobiocatalysis

Development of immobilised and nano-enabled enzyme systems to improve catalytic stability, recyclability and performance during biomass hydrolysis and environmental remediation.

Immobilisation Nanobiocatalyst Laccase Enzyme Stability
PROJECT 05

Lignin & Cellulose-Derived Functional Materials

Exploration of lignin and cellulose fractions as renewable precursors for nanomaterials, adsorbents and functional materials with potential applications in environmental remediation and sustainable material systems.

Lignin Nanocellulose Nanolignin Adsorbents Functional Materials
PROJECT 06

Integrated Sugar Beet Bagasse Biorefinery

Development of an integrated valorisation strategy for sugar beet bagasse combining bioethanol production with recovery and utilisation of residual biomass fractions for functional environmental materials.

Sugar Beet Bagasse Bioethanol Lignin Functional Materials Remediation
PROJECT 07

Laccase-Based Environmental Biotechnology

Application of free, immobilised and nano-enabled laccase systems for degradation of synthetic dyes and development of sustainable strategies for wastewater remediation.

Laccase Dye Degradation Wastewater Bioremediation
PROJECT 08

Sustainable Biorefinery Assessment

Integration of experimental biomass processing with process modelling, material balance, techno-economic analysis and life-cycle perspectives to identify scalable and sustainable conversion pathways.

TEA LCA Mass Balance Process Modelling Scale-Up
What We Do

Research Capabilities

Experimental and analytical capabilities supporting our biomass, enzyme and biorefinery research programme.

Capability 01

Biomass Pretreatment

Chemical, alkaline, microwave-assisted, surfactant-assisted and solvent-based biomass processing.

Capability 02

Biomass Compositional Analysis

Evaluation of cellulose, hemicellulose, lignin and related structural changes during biomass conversion.

Capability 03

Microbial Enzyme Production

Production and optimisation of cellulase, xylanase, pectinase, laccase and related industrial enzymes.

Capability 04

Enzyme Characterisation

Enzyme activity analysis, optimisation, stability assessment and kinetic characterisation.

Capability 05

Enzyme Immobilisation

Immobilisation of enzymes in matrices and development of reusable biocatalytic systems.

Capability 06

Enzymatic Saccharification

Evaluation and optimisation of enzymatic hydrolysis for conversion of structural carbohydrates into fermentable sugars.

Capability 07

Fermentation & Bioethanol

Fermentation process development, simultaneous saccharification and fermentation and bioethanol optimisation.

Capability 08

Statistical Process Optimisation

Application of response-surface methodologies, experimental design and statistical modelling to bioprocess optimisation.

Capability 09

Biomass Structural Characterisation

Interpretation of biomass structural transformations using complementary microscopic, spectroscopic and diffraction approaches.

Capability 10

Environmental Biocatalysis

Enzyme-based degradation of pollutants and development of biological systems for environmental remediation.

Capability 11

Techno-Economic Assessment

Process-flow development, mass-balance interpretation and assessment of economic feasibility for biomass conversion.

Capability 12

Life-Cycle Thinking

Integration of sustainability considerations into process evaluation and development of circular biorefinery strategies.
