Principal Investigator

Principal Investigator

Dr. Mohammad Ashfaque

Assistant Professor

Department of Biosciences
Integral University
Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India

His research focuses on lignocellulosic biomass valorisation, biomass pretreatment, industrial enzymes, bioethanol production, biocatalysis, lignin valorisation and integrated sustainable biorefineries.

The broader objective of the laboratory is to develop efficient biological and physicochemical approaches for converting renewable biomass into fuels, chemicals, enzymes and functional materials.
