Research Output

Publications

Peer-reviewed research contributions spanning lignocellulosic biomass valorisation, second-generation biofuels, biomass pretreatment, enzyme technology, biocatalysis, nanotechnology, environmental biotechnology and multidisciplinary global health collaborations.

Research Contributions

The publication portfolio reflects both the core research programme of LignoBio Lab and broader multidisciplinary collaborations. Core research focuses primarily on sustainable utilisation of lignocellulosic biomass, enzymatic bioconversion, second-generation bioethanol, biomass-derived materials and environmental biotechnology.

Multidisciplinary Collaboration

Global Health Collaborations

Through international collaborations, we contribute to large-scale global health research initiatives aimed at generating robust evidence to better understand disease burden, inform public health priorities, and support data-driven strategies for improving population health worldwide.
